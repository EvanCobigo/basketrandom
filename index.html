FLOPHOOPS

One button. Two floppy players. Total chaos.

WPlayer 1
↑Player 2
  • Press your key to hop. Both of your players jump together.
  • Hold to keep hopping and to raise your arm.
  • Touch the ball with a hand to grab it. Opponents can swipe it away.
  • Release your key while holding the ball to shoot. Shoot from close, upright and mid-jump for the best accuracy.
  • Carry the ball through the hoop to dunk. Every round changes the court, the ball and the players.
  • First to 5 wins. Esc/P pauses, M mutes. On touch screens, tap the left or right half.
